# TOFU Playbook — Top of Funnel

Contents: 1. Stage reality · 2. Psychological triggers · 3. Format matrix · 4. CTA rules · 5. Banned moves · 6. Patterns

---

## 1. Stage Reality

**Audience state**: Problem-unaware to problem-aware. They do not know your product, or they know of it but have no reason to care. Selling here is a conversion killer — the audience tunes out or scrolls past.

**Content job**: Create a memory trace. The audience finishes with a feeling ("this brand gets me") or a question ("wait, is that actually my problem?"). The product is present but never the point of the piece.

**Success signal**: Saves, shares, follows, comments — not clicks to a sales page.

---

## 2. Psychological Triggers

Use one primary trigger per piece. Mixing more than two dilutes the effect.

| Trigger | When to use | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Shared identity** | Niche community, lifestyle content | Names the audience's world with surgical precision so they feel seen before any product mention. Specificity is the differentiator — "freelance desainer yang burnout ngurusin revisi dari klien yang minta 'bikin lebih pop'" beats "people who work hard". |
| **Pattern interrupt** | Feed-scroll, video hook | The first 1–2 words or visual breaks the expected format — an unusual claim, an inverted assumption, an unexpected format. Stops the thumb before the brain decides to scroll. |
| **Insight reveal** | Educational carousel, thread, blog | Exposes a non-obvious truth that reframes the audience's understanding of their own problem. Not a tip — a perspective shift. The product becomes the logical conclusion, never the starting point. |
| **Vicarious experience** | Storytelling, short-form video | Places the audience inside a scenario they recognize. Emotion first — the product is visible but not the protagonist until ≥70% through or not at all. |
| **Curiosity gap** | Thread hook, headline, reel caption | Opens a genuine question the content will close. Gap must be resolvable by reading/watching — not manufactured clickbait. Test: can someone answer it after consuming the content? |

---

## 3. Format Matrix

| Format | Primary trigger | TOFU-specific execution |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram/TikTok short video | Pattern interrupt + vicarious | Open with the situation, not the product. Product visible but not named until ≥70% through, or end-card only. |
| Educational carousel | Insight reveal | Slide 1: provocative claim or framing. Slides 2–7: evidence, story, or perspective. Last slide: soft "save this" or "follow for more" — never a sales CTA. |
| Blog post | Insight reveal + SEO | Target problem-aware search terms (e.g., "kenapa keuangan UMKM selalu berantakan"). Product mention only in final 20%. |
| Twitter/X thread | Curiosity gap | Hook tweet contains the gap. Last tweet: link to deeper content or soft follow CTA — never to a sales page. |
| Podcast/YouTube | Shared identity | First 60 seconds names the exact audience persona with one concrete daily-life detail. |
| LinkedIn post | Insight reveal | Controversial opener (backed by evidence in the post). Industry-specific specificity. |

---

## 4. CTA Rules

**Allowed**: follow, save, share, "comment your answer", "tag someone who needs this", subscribe to newsletter/channel, download free resource.

**Forbidden**: "Buy now", "Book a call", pricing link, trial sign-up (unless the product is inherently free-entry and the CTA is the discovery, not the conversion).

**Why**: A purchase ask at TOFU signals to both the algorithm and the reader that this is an ad, not content. Both penalize it — the algorithm reduces reach, the reader exits.

---

## 5. Banned Moves

- Opening with the brand name or product name as the first words.
- Generic openers: "Halo guys", "Apakah kamu pernah merasa…", "Did you know…", "In today's fast-paced world…", "Siapa di sini yang…"
- Emoji walls (>3 emojis in the opening 3 lines).
- Manufactured urgency without a real constraint ("TERBATAS!", "Jangan sampai kelewatan!") — the audience has no reason to care yet.
- Fabricated social proof or statistics without a real source.
- Feature explanations, pricing, or product comparisons in the content body.
- Hashtag dumps: Instagram — 3–5 targeted hashtags, mix niche + local. More dilutes reach on most accounts.

---

## 6. Patterns

**Niche-identity opener** (caption/carousel):
`[Ultra-specific persona label]. [Their exact daily friction, named concretely — not a category, a scene]. [The non-obvious reframe that shifts the frame]. [Soft engagement invitation.]`

Bad: "Kamu kerja keras tapi hasilnya nggak sesuai ekspektasi?"
Good: "Kalau kamu desainer yang setiap pagi buka 14 tab sebelum mulai kerja, ini bukan soal manajemen waktu. Ini soal decision fatigue yang dibangun masuk ke workflow kamu."

**Curiosity-gap thread hook**:
`[Counterintuitive claim about their world]. Thread 🧵`
Rule: The claim must be fully resolvable by reading the thread — not by buying the product.

**Pattern-interrupt video hook** (first 3 seconds):
Start mid-action, mid-sentence, or with a statement that contradicts a common belief. The product is background context, not the subject.
